1

Make deleting a recipe hard to do by accident, and undoable

Do first

Two layers of protection. Moving Delete away stops the slip. The Undo toast and a 30-day Recently deleted list fix it when someone slips anyway. A confirm dialog alone is weaker, because people click through dialogs out of habit.

Proposed v1

  • Tick ingredients on a recipe, add them to one list
  • List grouped by recipe; tick off while shopping; Clear ticked
  • Add a free-text item (milk, bin bags)
  • Synced like recipes, so it is on your phone in the shop

Left for later

  • Merging duplicates (3 lemons + 2 lemons = 5). Needs quantity and unit parsing, which is the hard part
  • Grouping by aisle
  • Sharing a list with someone else
Your call: is v1 without duplicate merging good enough to ship? If users mostly cook one or two recipes a week, yes; if they plan whole weeks, merging may be what they are actually asking for.
